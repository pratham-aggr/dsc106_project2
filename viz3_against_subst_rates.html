The CCRB substantiates complaints at nearly equal rates regardless of complainant race

If the NYPD systematically targeted minorities, we would expect minority complaints to be more credible — substantiated at higher rates. Instead, White and Asian complainants have higher substantiation rates than Black complainants, and the differences are small. All figures computed directly from the ProPublica CCRB dataset.

What this means: The CCRB investigated every complaint in this dataset through a full process including civilian sworn statements. Across all racial groups, roughly one in four complaints was substantiated. White complainants had the second-highest rate at 27.9%, while Black complainants had the lowest at 23.8% , a gap of just 5.3 percentage points across all four groups. If police were disproportionately targeting minorities with unjustified stops and force, we would expect the opposite pattern: minority complaints should be substantiated more, not less.
Substantiation defined as any board_disposition beginning with "Substantiated". Includes: Charges, Command Discipline A/B, Formalized Training, Command Lvl Instructions, Instructions. Source: ProPublica / NYC Civilian Complaint Review Board · 33,358 records · Sep 1985 – Jan 2020.
