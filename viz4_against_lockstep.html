Substantiation rates rise and fall together across all races — the CCRB applies one standard

If investigators treated minority complaints differently, substantiation rates would diverge over time. Instead, all four racial groups track nearly identically across every five-year period — rising together, falling together — with a Black–Hispanic correlation of 0.99.

What this means: Substantiation rates spiked for every racial group in 2013–16 and bottomed out for every group in 2005–08. These shared swings reflect system-wide changes in CCRB investigative capacity, NYPD cooperation, and policy — not differential treatment of complainants by race. A correlation of r = 0.99 between Black and Hispanic substantiation rates over time is near-perfect. This is strong evidence that the review process applies a uniform standard regardless of who is filing the complaint.
