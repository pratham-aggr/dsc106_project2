Black New Yorkers have represented the majority of all CCRB complainants in every single four-year window since 2001 — never dropping below 58%. While the Black share has declined slightly in recent years as the Asian and White shares have grown, this reflects a shift in who is filing complaints, not a reduction in the fundamental disparity. Black residents still appear at 2.55× their population share as of the full dataset.
Only rows with known complainant ethnicity (Black / Hispanic / White / Asian) are included. Pre-2001 records are excluded: ethnicity data is absent for all records before 1996 and sparse through 2000 (n < 400/year).
